Writing assistance

Your Intelligent Academic Writing Companion

The composer works only with the information you provide. It organises, improves and structures — it does not fabricate experience. Every report varies based on your unique combination of school, mentor, dates, classes and reflections.

Variation per report

Each user receives a unique composer seed. Even two students from the same school will produce different prose across the entire document.

Contextual expansion

Chapters expand based on your host school, class list, subject combination, dates, and the challenges you documented.

Always reviewed by you

Generated text is a draft. You review, correct and approve every sentence before export.

The composer never invents: lessons that were never taught, school events that never happened, learner marks, mentor testimonies, official signatures, institutional approval, or references that do not exist. All generated text must be reviewed by the user.

Security notice. Access codes are never stored or checked in page source code. Verification is performed server-side against the authenticated user, the approved payment request and the report ownership. Codes are single-use and expire.

What users can never do

Privacy & security

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is REPORTX.COM?
REPORTX.COM is an AI-assisted internship report platform. It helps student teachers document real experience and organise it into a structured, academically formatted report — with variation generated per user so no two reports read the same.
2. Who can use REPORTX.COM?
Initially designed for student teachers, particularly TTC students in Rwanda. The architecture supports teacher-training institutions, universities, internship supervisors and other educational internship programmes.
3. Can I save my work before paying?
Yes. Creating an account, saving drafts, editing internship details and generating a preview are completely free. Payment only unlocks download in PDF/DOCX/HTML.
4. How does the AI composer work?
The composer reads only the information you enter — host school, mentors, dates, classes, subjects, activities, challenges, reflections. It restates that information into academic prose, varying the wording using a per-user seed so each report reads differently even when two students share a school. It never invents lessons, events or testimonies.
5. How does manual payment verification work?
You pay using the instructions published by the administrator, then submit your payment reference and report ID. An administrator verifies the payment independently and issues a unique access code. Enter the code to unlock download.
6. Can I edit my report at any time?
Yes. Every field remains editable. Your work is saved locally and to your account. You can regenerate the report after making changes.
7. Can I upload attachments like my timetable, scheme of work, mentor-stamped files, and lesson plans?
Yes. Every tab in the builder supports file attachments. Files are stored on your device via IndexedDB so the builder works offline, and images are embedded automatically as annex pages inside the generated report.
8. Can I download PDF, Word, or an offline copy?
PDF, DOCX and an offline HTML copy are all available after payment verification. A browser print button is also provided for direct A4 printing.
9. Is REPORTX.COM officially endorsed by REB?
No. REPORTX.COM is an independent platform. No official endorsement by the Rwanda Education Board or any other authority is claimed, and none should be assumed unless formally obtained and published.
10. Can my institution require a different report format?
Yes. The structure shown here is a configurable proposal. Institutions can define their own templates, headings and ordering. Final requirements and acceptance always remain subject to the relevant institution.
