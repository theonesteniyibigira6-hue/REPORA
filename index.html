Purpose

Designed for Real Academic and Professional Experiences

REPORA is built around genuine, documented internship activity — not generated fiction. It gives student teachers and internship participants a structured way to record what actually happened and present it professionally.

Student teachers

Document attachment periods, lessons taught, classroom observations and personal growth.

Teacher training colleges

Support cohorts with a consistent, configurable structure for internship documentation.

Internship supervisors

Review structured records of activities, mentor feedback and documented outcomes.

Educational institutions

Adopt institution-specific report templates and academic portfolio requirements.

Honest notice: REPORA is an independent report preparation platform. Final report requirements and acceptance remain subject to the relevant institution. REPORA does not issue, replace or guarantee institutional approval, certification or endorsement.

Writing assistance

Your Intelligent Academic Writing Companion

The assistant works only with the information you provide. It organises, improves and structures — it does not fabricate experience.

Structure & clarity

Organise user-provided information, convert notes into structured paragraphs and improve coherence.

Language support

Improve academic grammar, assist with formal English and help transform Kinyarwanda notes into English drafts.

Completeness checks

Suggest headings, identify missing report sections and check consistency of dates and names.

Summaries & reflections

Summarise documented events and suggest appropriate reflection framing based on what you recorded.

References

Organise the references you supply into a consistent citation layout.

Always reviewed by you

Generated text is a draft. You review, correct and approve every sentence before export.

The assistant never invents: lessons that were never taught, school events that never happened, learner marks, mentor testimonies, official signatures, institutional approval, or references that do not exist. All generated text must be reviewed by the user.

Access model

Simple, Transparent Report Access

REPORA uses manual payment verification. An administrator confirms your payment independently, then a unique access code is issued and verified by a trusted backend.

How access works

  1. 1Create an account.
  2. 2Complete the report information.
  3. 3Save your draft.
  4. 4Pay using the payment instructions displayed by the platform administrator.
  5. 5Submit your payment reference and report ID.
  6. 6Wait for payment verification.
  7. 7Receive your unique access code.
  8. 8Enter the code on the secure verification page.
  9. 9Unlock authorised report generation.
Go to Payment Verification
Security notice. Access codes are never stored or checked in page source code. Verification is performed server-side against the authenticated user, the approved payment request and the report ownership. Codes are single-use and expire.
Backend requirement. Payment verification and access-code issuance require a deployed trusted backend — Firebase Cloud Functions or an equivalent server. Until that backend is deployed and configured, this workflow is documented but not operational. The platform will display that state honestly rather than simulate an approval.

What users can never do

  • ✕ Set their own payment status to paid
  • ✕ Assign themselves administrator privileges
  • ✕ Reuse another user's access code
  • ✕ Read another user's report or documents

Privacy & security

Secure authentication

Owner-based access

User-controlled access

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is REPORA?

REPORA is an intelligent internship report creation and professional portfolio platform. It helps student teachers and internship participants document real experience and organise it into a structured, academically formatted report.

2. Who can use REPORA?

It is initially designed for student teachers, particularly TTC students in Rwanda. The architecture supports teacher-training institutions, universities, internship supervisors and other educational internship programmes.

3. Can I save my work before paying?

Yes. Creating an account and saving drafts, internship details and records is free. Payment only unlocks authorised report generation and export.

4. How do I generate my report?

Complete your internship information, save your draft, complete payment verification, then enter the access code issued to you. Generation runs on the trusted backend and produces your downloadable file.

5. How does manual payment verification work?

You pay using the instructions published by the administrator, then submit your payment reference and report ID. An administrator checks the payment independently, marks it verified, and a unique single-use access code is issued to your account.

6. Can I edit my report?

Yes. All information you enter remains editable in your account, and you can regenerate the report after making changes.

7. Can I download PDF?

PDF is a priority export format. It becomes available once the corresponding generator has been implemented and deployed, and once your report generation access is authorised.

8. Can I download Word?

DOCX is a priority export format for the initial release. As with PDF, availability depends on deployment of the generator and your access status.

9. Can I upload photographs?

Supporting documents and images can be attached as evidence to your events and appendix sections, stored privately in your own account space.

10. Can I use REPORA on mobile?

Yes. REPORA is built mobile-first and works on small smartphones, large smartphones, tablets, laptops and desktop monitors.

11. Is REPORA officially endorsed by REB?

No. REPORA is an independent platform. No official endorsement by the Rwanda Education Board or any other authority is claimed, and none should be assumed unless it has been formally obtained and published.

12. Can my institution require a different report format?

Yes. The structure shown on this page is a configurable proposal. Institutions can define their own templates, headings and ordering. Final requirements and acceptance always remain subject to the relevant institution.
